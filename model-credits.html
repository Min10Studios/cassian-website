Complete application source.

Cassian-1.2.1-Source.zip provides the matching application and dependency source, notices, local patches and build inputs. Cassian application source is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Source base: bab76f11cb3a9de1e953bf7633c3c748f3e17e68, plus the version, packaging and test-only overlays documented in SOURCE-RECIPE.json. GitHub’s automatic source archives contain only this website; use the explicit application-source asset for Cassian.

Model and IR source.

Cassian-1.0.0-Model-Source.zip is the reused source companion for the ordinary NAM captures and cabinet IRs. It is separate from application source; its original filename and bytes are retained. Download it alongside both of the other source companions. Use the 1.2.1 sound-bank notices for the separately licensed bundled captures and their attribution.

Third-party model captures in the model-source companion are credited to the Neural Amp Modeler community in pelennor2170/NAM_models, upstream revision 944ca6718581c60cc5365586d2f378d740e181f3, under the included GPL-3.0 terms. The separate Jester cabinet IRs use CC0-1.0. Equipment and contributor names are attribution, not endorsement; Cassian does not claim authorship of third-party captures.

Restored MIT model source.

Cassian-1.2.1-MIT-Model-Source.zip is the separate source companion for the six model files used by the eight restored presets. It includes source mapping, MIT licenses and contributor notices. Use the matching release notices for exact attribution. This companion does not replace the ordinary model/IR archive.
