Model and IR source.

Cassian-1.0.0-Model-Source.zip is the reused source companion for the ordinary NAM captures and cabinet IRs. It is separate from application source; its original filename and bytes are retained. Download it alongside both of the other source companions. Use the 1.3.1 sound-bank notices for the separately licensed bundled captures and their attribution.

Third-party model captures in the model-source companion are credited to the Neural Amp Modeler community in pelennor2170/NAM_models, upstream revision 944ca6718581c60cc5365586d2f378d740e181f3, under the included GPL-3.0 terms. The separate Jester cabinet IRs use CC0-1.0. Equipment and contributor names are attribution, not endorsement; Cassian does not claim authorship of third-party captures.

Restored MIT model source.

Cassian-1.2.2-MIT-Model-Source.zip is the separate source companion for the six model files used by the eight restored presets. It includes source mapping, MIT licenses and contributor notices. Use the matching release notices for exact attribution. This companion does not replace the ordinary model/IR archive.

Uriel amp and Governor cabinet.

Uriel uses an amp-only capture of a Bogner Uber Ultra KT88, credited in the supplied model metadata to dididishe, paired with the supplied Eminence Governor 4×12 / SM57 cabinet recordings. Redistribution rights were confirmed by the founder for these purchased assets. They retain separate terms and are not relicensed as GPL, MIT or CC0. See the release sound-bank notices and bundled Uriel notice for details.

Uriel uses original Cassian artwork. Equipment names identify the captured hardware and do not imply affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement.
