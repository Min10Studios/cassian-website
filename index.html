CASSIAN 1.1.0

What’s new in 1.1.0.

  • Four illustrated heads with 13 curated starting sounds.
  • Four featured capture pedals with distinct artwork and creator credits.
  • Remove pedals from Tone or Pedalboard and restore the previous board with Undo.

Known issueAdding Drive may cause feedback. If it happens, bypass Drive and lower your monitoring level.

FEATURED CAPTURE PEDALS

Choose your drive.

Each featured capture has its own Cassian artwork and Input, Output and Bypass controls.

Make room on the board.

Use Remove on a pedal card in Tone or Pedalboard. Undo restores the previous board. EQ, delay and reverb retain their own controls.

BAND / PRACTICE

Give the riff a rhythm section.

Play with drums and bass. Choose a groove, set the root key and tonality, and type a manual tempo from 40 to 240 BPM or tap it in.

Change the feel as you play.

Adjust key, mode, bass movement and groove while the Band plays. Musical changes take effect at the next bar; drum and bass levels respond immediately. Recording and preparation can temporarily lock controls.

Take the arrangement with you.

Export the saved Band arrangement as MIDI, with an optional matching WAV. Apply your arrangement edits before export, then use the completed files in your DAW.

LEARN / TONE CRAFTING ACADEMY

Learn the part.
Shape the sound.

Explore scales and chord shapes on the interactive fretboard, choose a lesson key, and work through genre pathways. The circle of fifths and transposed progressions keep the theory close to the guitar.

Open the learning room ↗

Try the tone.

Use the existing tone recipes to explore how amp, cabinet and pedal choices work together.

Play with Band.

Send the lesson’s available groove and key to Band, then press Play when you’re ready. Cassian asks before replacing a custom arrangement.

WINDOWS STANDALONE

Cassian 1.1.0

Download the free Windows standalone installer. Cassian runs offline after installation. This installer is unsigned. Windows may show a publisher warning.

Updating from Cassian 1.0.0 Beta? Your old profile files stay on disk. Cassian 1.1.0 uses a separate profile, so old settings and library entries do not carry over automatically.

Before you download.
