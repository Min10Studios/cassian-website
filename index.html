CASSIAN 1.3.1 · OUT NOW

A new voice. A commanding presence.

Introducing Uriel, the newest member of the Cassian amp family.

High gain. Your way.

Start with the new Uriel rig, paired with the Governor 4×12 / SM57 cabinet. Explore all seven supplied microphone positions in Cabinets to find the character that suits your playing.

Ivory covering, a brushed silver grille, gold chicken-head controls and cyan-white tube glow give this head its own identity.

  • A new illustrated amp head. Uriel joins Ferrum, Bastion, Rubicon and Viridian.
  • Seven cabinet perspectives. Select a Governor 4×12 SM57 mic position in Cabinets.
  • Reliable capture loading and closing. Uriel’s cabinet loads correctly, and Cassian can close normally if a capture cannot load.
  • Your studio stays with you. Pedals, Band, recording, Learn and the earlier sound choices remain available offline.

Ferrum remains the default for a fresh start. Your saved rigs keep their sound settings. Uriel is a high-gain rig; if idle noise is intrusive, bypass it between takes. Gain staging and gating refinements are planned next.

FEATURED CAPTURE PEDALS

Shape every part of your sound.

Seven-band EQ and refreshed drives, delay, reverb and modulation give you more ways to shape the rig. Featured pedal captures retain their own Cassian artwork, Input, Output and Bypass controls, and creator credits.

Make room on the board.

Use Remove on a pedal card in Tone or Pedalboard. Undo restores the previous board. EQ, delay and reverb retain their own controls.

BAND / PRACTICE

Give the riff a rhythm section.

Play along offline, shape a groove or build multi-bar sections into an arrangement. Add swing, change intensity and let the bass follow the harmony.

Cue the next bar.

Use the Band’s feel and intensity controls to give a part more space or more drive. Cue section and energy changes while the music plays, or build sections in Song Maker.

Choose the bass tuning while keeping the song’s key. Chord-aware patterns, approach notes and walking lines offer different ways to support the guitar.

Take the arrangement with you.

Export the saved Band arrangement as MIDI, with an optional matching WAV. Apply your arrangement edits before export, then use the completed files in your DAW.
