Know your learning tools.

The website brings Cassian’s learning material to a browser. It is a separate browser experience; it does not run Cassian’s amp models, effects engine or native Band player.

Local-first learning.

No account, subscription or cloud connection is needed for these tools after the page and its assets have loaded. Preferences and manually marked lesson progress are stored in this browser when storage is available; otherwise the tools use temporary in-memory state. They do not sync with Cassian or another device. No private songs, paid tabs, recordings or amp-model banks are part of this page.

Reference audio.

Notes, chord arpeggios and the practice pulse use synthetic Web Audio started by your action. They are learning references, not guitar amp sounds, microphone analysis or the native Band engine. If audio or clipboard access is unavailable, the tool reports that limitation. It does not upload or record your playing.
